lncFit findings log

What we tried for predicting lncRNA essentiality in a cell line the model has never seen, and what we learned. Newest step first. This replaces the leaderboard: no rankings, no submissions.

Ground rules

  • One step at a time. Each step is one small PR, reviewed before the next starts.
  • Evaluation holds out the cell line and the genes. Each training line (HAP1, K562, MDA-MB-231) is held out in turn, and the genes being predicted are left out of training too (5 gene folds, lncfit/honest_eval.py). Holding out only the cell line lets a model score by recognising genes it saw labelled in the other lines.
  • Results are reported per cell line, not only as a mean. A change that helps one line and hurts another is not a finding.
  • Features not used: measured depletion (fold_change, rra_pvalue, anything derived), THP1 labels, and guide_count / S1B guide-design columns, which predict the label through how it is computed (RRA over the top 5 guides; genes with 4 guides were never called essential).

AUPRC, fitted candidates averaged over 3 gene-split seeds.

Findings

  • Nothing fitted beats the sort. The best fitted model, logistic regression on mRNA alone, ties it (0.114).
  • The signal is all in the top decile. Among expressed genes, the hit rate is flat for the first nine mRNA deciles and jumps only in the top one (0.105–0.124, 3–4× the base rate). AUPRC is decided by ordering within that decile.
  • Tree models throw that ordering away. A monotone XGBoost on mRNA alone should rank exactly like the sort, yet it scores 0.098: it gives the 550 most-expressed genes only 35–38 distinct scores. Ties at the top cost about 0.017.
  • “Max of total, mRNA” is not adopted. Its mean gain (+0.008) is almost all MDA-MB-231, and it is slightly worse on K562, so it fails the every-line rule.

Decision: later steps add features through plain logistic regression on mRNA expression, which keeps fine ordering and matches the sort. A feature group is kept only if it beats that base on every cell line.

Findings

  • Expression alone is a real signal. Ranking by the gene's own mRNA expression gets 2.3× the random baseline, on all three lines, with nothing fitted.
  • Fitting a model on the two expression columns made things worse. Total-RNA and mRNA TPM are strongly correlated (ρ 0.74–0.80), and logistic regression gives total-RNA TPM a negative weight in 2 of 3 folds. That contrast does not hold in a new cell line: MDA-MB-231 drops from 0.120 to 0.072.
  • Adding the S1A gene annotations does nothing (0.089 → 0.087) in a linear model.
  • The previous 39-feature XGBoost (#118 minus guide_count) scores 0.101 under this evaluation, below the mRNA sort. Its 39 features and tuning add nothing over one expression column once gene recognition is removed.

Bar for step 2: 0.115 (mRNA rank), on every cell line. A fitted model that cannot beat a sorted column is not learning anything that transfers.
