details

True four-fold leave-one-cell-line-out prediction for Day 14. Each fold uses the same lncRNA's gene-level and individual-guide Day-14 depletion measurements from the three training cell lines, together with pre-screen annotations, cell-specific expression, transcript sequence, and Cas13 guide-design features. Guide summaries include depletion order statistics, replicate agreement, and the fraction of consistently depleted guides. The held-out cell line's labels, p-values, fold changes, and guide outcomes are never used by that fold. Day-0 and Day-7 measurements are never loaded. The final prediction is a fixed min-max-normalized blend of five pseudohuber XGBoost depletion regressors (depths 1, 3, and 5). Its strongest component explicitly matches source count: a training row sees two other training lines, and each held-out prediction is averaged across the three possible two-source views. Fixed weights are 0.36 expression-matched guide-depth3, 0.11 source-specific gene-depth1, 0.14 expression-only-depth1, 0.18 full guide-depth5, and 0.21 full guide-depth3. Overall LOCO performance is AUROC 0.7781 and AUPRC 0.2186, up from this submission's previous 0.7332/0.1784. Carried over from the retired 4-fold leave-one-cell-line-out challenge, keeping only its THP1 rows. That fold trained on HAP1/K562/MDA-MB-231 with THP1's labels, p-values, fold changes and guide outcomes all withheld, which is exactly this challenge's protocol, so the score transfers unchanged.
